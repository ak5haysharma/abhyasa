# Changelog

- Adaptive swaps: unlimited swaps with a reason, 'I did something else' (keeps your streak, can be saved as a task), suggestions that learn from what you finish and skip, and a 'What Abhyasa has learned' section on the Hero tab. The swap skill perk is now an extra streak shield.

- Locked the app to the screen: no pinch or double-tap zoom, no sideways scrolling.

- First release as an installable app: Today, Journey and Hero tabs.
- Questionnaire with kit, weekly routine, daily time and a streak goal.
- Daily check-in, tailored tasks with guides and built-in tools.
- Streaks, morning story deck, travelling companion, charms, evening close.
- Hindu seasons, festivals (Nov 2026 to Oct 2027) and daily anecdotes.
- Offline support, update prompt, backup and restore.
