# Changelog

## 1.12.0 · 24.11.2026
- Four new paths: Friendship (Krishna), Money (Lakshmi), Courage (Durga) and Wisdom (Nachiketa), each with portraits, 5 skills, trials, about 18 tasks, guide intros and 4 journey chapters. Also fixes the light-mode colour contrast from 1.10, which hadn't applied.

## 1.11.0 · 24.11.2026
- Task library nearly doubled (109 to 203), with every path now at 22 or more tasks with guides and tools. The planner remembers the last 7 days to avoid repeats, and short tasks rest for 3 days after appearing.

## 1.10.0 · 24.11.2026
- Reliability: fonts bundled in the app (no Google requests, fully offline), a Send feedback sheet (Share, Copy, optional Email), a weekly backup reminder and iPhone-friendly backup via the share sheet, colour contrast raised to accessibility standard, and better keyboard and screen-reader focus in sheets.

## 1.9.0 · 24.11.2026
- Trial ceremony: your guide asks how it felt and what helped, then an honest 'Did you truly pass?', then a skill-unlock animation with a chime and a reply from your guide. Reflections are kept on each skill. Check-in feedback: after 'Fit my plan', a summary of what was taken out, what was added and why, and which routines to take carefully.

## 1.8.0 · 24.11.2026
- Explainer pack: a Learn hub with 5 two-minute swipe guides (How Abhyasa works, The Ramayana, The Mahabharata, The old texts, Living the journey), a Who's who of about 35 figures, and the glossary. A Start here card sits on Today until you've finished the guides, and new users get the tour after setup.

## 1.7.0 · 24.11.2026
- Focus mode: start any task full screen, with a countdown ring around your guide, steps to tick off, the task's tool, a new guide line every 45 seconds, pause and resume, the screen kept awake, a chime at the end, and +5 min or Mark done.

## 1.6.1 · 24.11.2026
- Tree explorer: tapping near any star, bridge or portrait selects it, taps are more forgiving, and locked and in-progress skills show how much growth is left and a time estimate at your current pace.

## 1.6.0 · 24.11.2026
- Explore the skill tree like a game: full-screen, drag to pan, pinch or +/- to zoom, tap any star and the camera glides to it, with a detail panel for each skill, bridge, guide and your rank. Arrows walk along a branch; Next branch jumps across.

## 1.5.2 · 24.11.2026
- More reliable updates: new versions always download fresh files, and the app checks for updates each time you open it.

## 1.5.1 · 24.11.2026
- New skill tree: a night-sky constellation with glowing branches, energy flowing along unlocked skills, Devanagari skill letters, guide portraits at branch tips, gold bridges and a turning lotus mandala at the centre.

## 1.5.0 · 24.11.2026
- New layout: Today has activity rings, a swipeable card strip and a compact task list with guides in a sheet. Journey has Paths, Tree and Calendar tabs, with path cards showing progress and 7-day sparklines. Hero has Hero, Stats, Collection and More tabs, with a 7-day chart and a 5-week heatmap.

## 1.4.0 · 24.11.2026
- Weekly review: every Sunday (or until Tuesday), five swipe cards covering active days, minutes, moods, time per path, best day, a guide's comment, 'worth it?' for your own tasks, and next week's focus, which gets more daily time. Past reviews on the Hero tab.

## 1.3.0 · 24.11.2026
- Starter pack: 'How well do you know these stories?' in onboarding, 3-card guide intros, English meanings next to Sanskrit names, a glossary on the Hero tab, and first-time tips on each tab.

## 1.2.0 · 24.11.2026
- Adaptive swaps: unlimited swaps with a reason, 'I did something else' (keeps your streak, can be saved as a task), suggestions that learn from what you finish and skip, and a 'What Abhyasa has learned' section on the Hero tab. The swap skill perk is now an extra streak shield.

## 1.1.1 · 24.11.2026
- Locked the app to the screen: no pinch or double-tap zoom, no sideways scrolling.

## 1.1.0 · 23.11.2026
- Five realms and 8 paths: added Flexibility and breath (Patanjali), Partner and love (Savitri), Learning (Saraswati), Career focus (Arjuna) and Meditation (Dhruva). Choose up to 5 focus paths, add or pause paths any time, readiness suggestions, realm balance wheel.

## 1.0.0 · 23.11.2026
- First release as an installable app: Today, Journey and Hero tabs.
- Questionnaire with kit, weekly routine, daily time and a streak goal.
- Daily check-in, tailored tasks with guides and built-in tools.
- Streaks, morning story deck, travelling companion, charms, evening close.
- Hindu seasons, festivals (Nov 2026 to Oct 2027) and daily anecdotes.
- Offline support, update prompt, backup and restore.
